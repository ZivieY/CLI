Our Mission

Around the world, children and adolescents in underserved communities continue to face barriers to achieving proficient literacy. Children's Literacy Impact recognizes this pressing challenge and is committed to creating meaningful change through literature, art, and creative expression.

Through our literacy projects and creative initiatives, we strive to make complex ideas more accessible, encourage young people to engage with literature, and provide artists and writers with opportunities to share their work.

Artistry matters in the pursuit of spreading literacy.

What We Do

Literacy Projects

Programs and creative projects that use writing, illustration, and other forms of art to communicate ideas and encourage expression.

Complex Ideas, Made Accessible

Simplifying complicated or heavy topics into approachable literary and creative experiences.

Art & Storytelling

Sharing writing, illustration, and other forms of art to spread awareness.

Bunny Tales

Our creative publication featuring stories, poetry, artwork, and educational pieces from our community.
